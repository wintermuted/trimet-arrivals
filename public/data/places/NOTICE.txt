Sources:

Changes: selected Portland-addressed Oregon records (including nearby
unincorporated areas); excluded non-library facilities from the library layer;
normalized names, addresses, website links, and approximate point locations.
Community centers include public-serving nonprofit/social facilities and may
have eligibility requirements or fees. Library source points are older.
Reviewed corrections (2026-11-18): replaced obsolete Albina and Northwest
addresses/coordinates using the current official branch pages and their TriMet
coordinate links:
https://multcolib.org/hours-and-locations/albina-library
https://multcolib.org/hours-and-locations/northwest-library

https://multcolib.org/hours-and-locations/hillsdale-library
Recheck those decisions on each manual refresh. Neither source records nor
reviewed corrections establish current access, hours, or a verified entrance.

Each snapshot carries its actual retrieval date and, when supplied, source
database date. Retrieval does not mean that every feature was recently verified.
None of these datasets claim verified public entrances. All initial routing handoffs
use approximate locations and require confirmation or correction in the planner.
